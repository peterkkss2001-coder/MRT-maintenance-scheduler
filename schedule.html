Report a Problem — {{ current_sector }}

Staff, day and time are picked automatically: eligible staff by problem type, day/time by nearest available slot based on importance.

Pair Up Uncovered Staff

Scans every sector, every role, every day of the week (Mon-Sun). Anyone with no shift on a given day gets paired onto the SAME sector/time as a same-role staff member who already has one that day, so nobody is left with nothing to do. Anyone who already has their own shift that day is left untouched.

Report Emergency

Flags the shift with a big icon, and automatically flags a paired partner's shift too. Choosing "Add More Time" also adds a brand-new shift right after this one for this person only (if there's free time) - the original shift is never changed. Any other type only flags.

Clear All Shifts

Deletes every shift in every sector (A, B and C) for the whole week. This cannot be undone - use it to start the schedule fresh.
